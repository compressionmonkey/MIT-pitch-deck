The traps

High scientific value, but wrong for a builder who needs a revenue path within the first year.

Sculpting Evolution

Biotech platforms. Needs a lab, 5–10 year timelines, regulatory patience. Think Ginkgo Bioworks, not a weekend hack.

Personal Robots

Hardware is brutal. Jibo died; iRobot got restructured in 2026. Avoid unless I have a very specific vertical and deep hardware expertise.

Molecular Machines

Pharma and medical devices. Ultra-high value but requires heavy lab infrastructure and FDA navigation.

Nano-Cybernetic Biotrek

Brain implants and cancer therapeutics. Fascinating, but heavily biomedical. More cyborg than ALife.

I will revisit these if I ever have a biotech cofounder and a Series A in hand. Until then, software only.

My leaning

I am strongly leaning toward Viral Communications as my first choice. It is the cleanest intersection of:

  • Multi-agent coordination — my core ALife interest
  • Pure software — my builder speed
  • A real market problem — information overload + siloed platforms
  • A clear spinoff path — the group already thinks in products

Human Dynamics is my backup if Viral Communications does not have an open slot. Fluid Interfaces is a distant third — only if I decide to prioritize consumer speed over research depth.
